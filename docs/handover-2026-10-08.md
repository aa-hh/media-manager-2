# Handover, 8 Oct 2026

Where media-manager-2 planning and design stand, and what to do next.

## The product

A web app over Sonarr and Radarr for one power user. It shows live rTorrent status, fixes failed or stuck downloads by itself, and lets the owner replace a file or keep a second version. It warns about hit and run rules before anything is removed, and adds health checks, a calendar and Plex sign-in. Friend requests are v2, and playback statistics are out of v1. `PRODUCT.md` and the glossary `CONTEXT.md` hold the detail. "Release" always means a torrent.

## Design (PR #3)

- `design/mediaManager2.pen` holds the design as 26 pen.dev screens (25 desktop, 1 phone), and is the source of truth. pen.dev keeps edits in memory until the owner presses Cmd+S in the app. Check the file on disk changed before committing it.
- Every screen was critiqued with impeccable (`docs/design/critiques/`), then decluttered. The rules that came out of that are in `.impeccable/surfaces/design-mediamanager2-pen.md` and the "Revised 8 Oct" line of `docs/design/sonarr-radarr-review.md`:
  - Show a state only when it is unusual.
  - Purple only on the one best release that passes the quality profile; red for any problem or risk.
  - The live downloads bar is one line.
  - Row actions appear on the selected row only.
  - One inline panel open at a time, with the rest of the screen dimmed.
  - Destructive actions take two steps.
  - A key means one thing per screen.
- Keyboard shortcuts are a nice-to-have, not a requirement (`PRODUCT.md`).
- `docs/design/sonarr-radarr-review.md` records what was kept, changed or dropped from Sonarr's and Radarr's own screens.
